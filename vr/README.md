# VR Library

3D cubes of the learner's space (z, ż, P), to explore on a desktop or in a VR headset.
Each cube shows the transition bands the SKA learner registers from one ECG record.

**Application:** virtual reality in cardiology with an AI assistant. The cardiologist
navigates the bands with a headset; the assistant measures what the selected cycles share.

## Cubes

PhysioNet MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database, lead MLII, 360 Hz, scale 25. Four cubes per record: the cube (P), the same with the steps into or out of a missing wave tagged (×), the cube with Δt, the time since the previous wave, as the vertical axis, and the tagged cube with the MIT-BIH labels (in the title; click a point to read its transition and the label of its cycle). Open a cube and press **Enter VR** in the headset browser.

τ_H is the entropic characteristic time of the heart, defined in [`ska-ecg-engine/`](../ska-ecg-engine/).
